# C · Fireside Yard

Plan: `plan-c.jpg` (lake at the top, grid 4 m). Coordinates are metres on the map (x east, z toward town).

**The idea.** The camp is a fenced yard in town, at the quay's east end, where the quay road, the lane up to the
square and the lane down to the point already meet. It is a village fireside: you pass through it on the east loop,
and it is eight seconds from the Smokehouse.

**Site and size.** The flat grass between the quay's east end, Pip's fish racks and the wooded bank, which is empty
today except for two lanes. A cobbled circle 8.4 m across at (18.6, 16.6) inside a fence about 15 m across, plus a
strand and a jetty on the cove's east bank, 6 m out of its north gate. No ground is cut: the site is level.

**Arriving.** The quay road no longer bends away at the deck's end: it runs straight on, past the racks and the
Legends' Totem, under the ANGLERS' REST arch and into the yard. A picket gate on the south side takes the lane up to
the well square; one on the north takes the lane down to the strand and on to the point.

**The centrepiece.** The Great Fire, a stone hearth 2.1 m across, under a crown of lights on eight tall posts. From
the pier's root you look along the quay at the arch with the fire and its crown behind it; from the pier's end it
shows over the quay's east corner, beside the stalls' lights.

**Seats (21).** Six log seats for two round the hearth, set so the three ways in pass between them, each with its
instrument. A stone seat wall along the bank side (9 seats). Lake Legends and Deep Sea stand on the bank behind it,
each with its record fish mounted over it, facing the fire, the quay and the pier, with a forest pine between them.

**Bram's Kettle.** The host's stall stands in the yard's south-west corner, facing the fire, a butter-yellow shack
with a striped awning and a lit lantern. Its back is to the racks and the Smokehouse, so the town's three counters
(Marlow, Mags, Bram) stand in a row along the waterfront. Thank You stands beside it; the Derby stand is by the arch.

**The Strand.** Out of the north gate, six metres down the lane: a pebble beach on the cove's east bank with the stone
pile, the skip sign and a bench, and a rocket jetty 1.6 m by 4.9 m out into the cove with the rack at its end. Rockets
go up over the cove, in view of the yard, the quay and the pier.

**Edges.** The town's white picket fence all the way round, broken only by the arch and the two gates, with a hedge
behind the boards, flower beds inside the fence and two leafy trees planted at its south corners.

**The old fire.** The small campfire on the point stays as it is, without the boards: a quiet spot for two across the
water, still seen from the jetty bench.

**Night.** Lights that cast: the Great Fire, the stall's lantern, and the old fire. The crown and the gates glow.

**What moves.** The quay road's east arm and the east ring lane are re-laid to meet at the yard; two lamp posts move
to the gates; the boards come over from the point. The racks, the Smokehouse and the Net Mender's stay.

**Building it.** One round level on flat ground, one dock rectangle, a ring of fence. New props: hearth, stall, totem,
rocket rack, hedge, flat stones. The least earthwork of the three.

**Risks.** The water is not at the fire: stones and rockets are a short walk out of the gate. Three lanes cross the
circle, so players walk through it. It takes the cove's east bank, 5 m from the east jetty.
