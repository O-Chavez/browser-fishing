# A · Ember Point

Plan: `plan-a.jpg` (lake at the top, grid 4 m). Coordinates are metres on the map (x east, z toward town).

**The idea.** The camp is a small open-air theatre on the east headland, facing the cove. One round terrace holds
everything: the Great Fire in the middle, seats in the round, and a back wall that is the camp's face (Deep Sea, the
host's stall, Lake Legends). It is the cove's beacon: every player on the pier, the jetties and the quay looks straight
at it.

**Site and size.** The headland where the small campfire is today. A cobbled terrace 9.2 m across at (19.8, 3.0), a
pebble beach on the point's lake side, and a short jetty off the tip. About 12 m by 19 m in all. The ridge behind is
cut back about a metre and held by a stone retaining wall.

**Arriving.** The campfire lane from the quay's east end is unchanged. Where the lanes meet (17.4, 9.4) a signpost
points the way, and three steps on you pass under a timber arch, ANGLERS' REST, with a lantern on each post. The
Derby stand faces the lane beside the arch; Thank You stands just inside.

**The centrepiece.** The Great Fire: a stone hearth 2.1 m across with flames to head height, about twice today's fire.
Above it a crown of lights: one ring of bulbs strung round seven tall posts on the terrace's rim. By day the posts and
the back wall make the outline; at night the crown and the fire are one lit ring over the water.

**Seats (20).** Six log seats for two round the hearth, 2.5 m out, with a gap toward the cove so nobody sits with
their back to the pier's view. Behind them the stone seat wall in two arcs (8 seats). Each log has its instrument
standing by it (banjo, drum, squeezebox, whistle, washboard, jug): sit there and you play it.

**The back wall.** Between the seat wall's arcs stands Bram's Kettle, the host's stall: a butter-yellow clapboard
shack 3.2 m wide with a striped awning, a kettle on the counter and a crate of rockets, in the family of Marlow's
stall. Bram Tinder, the fire keeper, sells the rockets. Either side of it, behind the seat wall, Lake Legends (quay
side) and Deep Sea (lake side), each with its record fish mounted over the board. Behind all three the retaining wall,
a hedge on its top and the town's white picket fence, which runs on down to the gate and to the beach, so the camp is
closed on the hill side and open to the water.

**Skipper's Beach.** Two steps down from the terrace's lake side: a pebble beach on the point, about 7 m by 5 m,
facing the open lake. A pile of flat stones, the skip sign, a bench under the headland pine, and the Legends' Totem
at its tip: carved fish stacked 3 m high, one for each lake legend with the Kraken on top, standing where the pier
sees it against the water.

**The rocket jetty.** A timber jetty 1.6 m by 5.2 m from the beach out over the open lake, with the rocket rack and a
lantern at its end. Rockets go up from there, 13 m from the nearest seat, over open water, in full view of the
terrace, the pier and the quay. It is a landing, not a fishing spot.

**Edges.** Hill side: wall, hedge, fence. Cove side: a rope rail along the rim with one gap, where steps go down to
the sand under a lantern. Lake side: rope rail either side of the beach steps.

**Night.** Lights that cast: the fire and the stall's lantern. Everything else glows: the crown, the gate's lanterns,
the jetty's lantern.

**What moves.** The second headland pine goes 5 m north-east, behind the fence. Nothing else in town moves. Bottle
"Warm hands by the fire, cold water at my feet" ends up on the beach.

**Building it.** One round level, one cut with a wall, one dock rectangle. New props: the hearth, the stall (Marlow's
shack re-proportioned), totem, rope rail, rocket rack, hedge, flat stones. No forest tree is touched.

**Risks.** It is the smallest of the three: twelve players fill the circle. The terrace reaches about a metre further
into the cove than today's ring.
