# B · Tarn Camp (round 2)

Plan: `plan-b.jpg` (lake at the top, grid 4 m). Coordinates are metres on the map (x east, z toward town). Round 1's
version is in `round1/`.

**The idea.** A sheltered camp behind a stone rampart on the headland, with its own small pond, the tarn, at its back.
From the cove you see one gateway under an arch with the fire framed in it; inside, the circle is closed by the
rampart, a hedge and the water.

**What changed since round 1.** One fire, not two: the beacon brazier is gone. The thin ridge is built as a rampart
with a wide gateway instead of a narrow cut. Skipping stones moved from the tarn to the Landing, on the cove. The
circle grew from 8.4 m to 11 m and its log ring opened up. The boards came down to floor level. The far bank is a
designed rock bank, and the rowboat is gone.

**Site and size.** From the cove's east shore to the tarn's far bank: about 25 m by 21 m. The fire circle is 11 m
across at (28.6, 2.4), its floor cut up to 1.2 m into the back of the ridge. The tarn (a natural puddle today) is dug
to about 5 m by 8.5 m of water, right against the circle's east side.

**The rampart and the gateway.** The strip of ridge left between the cove and the circle is 2 m thick: too thin to
leave as a bank. It is built as a rampart: stone on both faces, 1.2 m high, with a hedge on top to head height. One
gateway 3.6 m wide goes through it under the ANGLERS' REST arch, with a lit lantern on the arch. The gateway is
sighted on the pier: from where players spawn you look across the cove through the arch at the Great Fire, with the
crown of lights (4 m up) showing over the hedge (2.8 m). On the rampart's cove face, either side of the arch, hang the
two record fish with their holders' names, so what the best anglers have done faces the cove.

**The Landing.** In front of the gateway, at the water where today's fire is: a cobbled round 5.2 m across. It is the
stones place: a stone pile and the skip sign on its cove rim, with open water to throw across, two log seats 3 m from
the pile, and the Legends' Totem (the lake's legends below, the sea's above, the Kraken on top) where the pier sees
it. The lane from the quay arrives here past a signpost (Fire, Stones, Rockets).

**The back lane.** A second cobbled lane leaves the same meeting of lanes, comes round the ridge's foot past the Derby
stand and Thank You, and enters the circle on its south side. The camp is a loop, not a dead end.

**The centrepiece.** The Great Fire, a stone hearth 2.1 m across, under a crown of lights on nine tall posts, with
the tarn right behind it, so from the gateway the fire is seen with its reflection.

**Measures of the circle.** Hearth, five log seats for two at 2.9 m, a clear ring 1.8 m wide all round behind the
logs, then the rim. The log ring has openings of 3 m on the gateway's line, the tarn's side and the back lane's line.

**Seats (20 at the fire, 4 on the Landing).** The five logs (10), each with its instrument. Stone seat wall along the
north rim and the south-east rim (10).

**The boards.** Lake Legends and Deep Sea stand on the rim at floor level, each in its own gap with no seat in front,
either side of the gateway, each with its record fish above. They are read from the clear ring.

**The tarn.** The circle's east rim is a stone kerb at the water. The fuse post stands at the kerb's middle, 3.5 m from the
nearest seat; the rocket raft is moored out on the tarn. Rockets go up from the water in front of everyone at the
fire, 6 m from the nearest seat, and show over the trees from town. The far bank is a rock bank about 2 m high with reeds at its foot,
built from the boulders the lake's shore already uses: a retaining line, not a bare slope.

**Bram's Kettle.** The host's stall stands off the back lane at the circle's south-east, under the big leafy tree
that already grows there, its counter turned to the fire, with a lit lantern and a patch of lawn. Bram Tinder sells
three rockets: a small one, a big one and a finale priced for the richest anglers (the prices are Oscar's).

**Edges.** Cove side: the rampart. North: the retaining wall with the hedge on top, running on to the tarn's rock
bank, and three pines planted behind it. East: the tarn and its rock bank. South: one run of white picket fence along
the back lane and round the stall's lawn.

**Prompts.** Seats, boards and the stall each ask the player to face them, as the casino's exit does. The fuse post,
the boards, the counter and the stone pile are each 3 m or more from one another.

**Night.** Lights that cast: the Great Fire, the arch's lantern and the stall's lantern.

**Building it.** One round level cut into the ridge, a pond, a lawn. The rampart, the gateway and the rock bank are
props standing over the terrain's slopes, which the coarse mesh cannot shape that finely. The most earthwork and the
most props of the three. Both forest trees on the site stay.

**Risks.** From the quay you see the gateway, the fire in it and the crown, not the seats. It is 10 m further from the
quay than today's fire.
