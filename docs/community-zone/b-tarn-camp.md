# B · Tarn Camp (round 3)

Plan: `plan-b.jpg` (lake at the top, grid 4 m). Coordinates are metres on the map (x east, z toward town). Earlier
versions are in `round1/` and `round2/`.

**What changed since round 2** (4 of 5 passed it; the phone player did not). The seat wall that stood in front of
Bram's counter is gone: 4 m of rim are open there. The circle's tarn side is a low kerb wall, so the clear ring does
not depend on where the dug shoreline lands. From the polish notes: the Landing's two logs are in its north half,
off the path and the pier's line, with the stones on its west rim and the Totem at its tip; the record fish hang on
the arch's posts above head height; the second headland pine moves up the ridge, off the rampart; a planted bed
fills the triangle between the two lanes; the hedge joins the rock bank; the rock bank is stacked the full 2 m; the
hedge's top steps between 2.2 m and 2.8 m so the arch stays the tallest thing; a cast at the tarn is answered.

**The idea.** A sheltered camp behind a stone rampart on the headland, with its own small pond, the tarn, at its back.
From the cove you see one gateway under an arch with the fire framed in it; inside, the circle is closed by the
rampart, a hedge and the water.

**What changed since round 1.** One fire, not two: the beacon brazier is gone. The thin ridge is built as a rampart
with a wide gateway instead of a narrow cut. Skipping stones moved from the tarn to the Landing, on the cove. The
circle grew from 8.4 m to 11 m and its log ring opened up. The boards came down to floor level. The far bank is a
designed rock bank, and the rowboat is gone.

**Site and size.** From the cove's east shore to the tarn's far bank: about 25 m by 21 m. The fire circle is 11 m
across at (28.6, 2.4), its floor cut up to 1.2 m into the back of the ridge. The tarn (a natural puddle today) is dug
to about 5 m by 8.5 m of water, right against the circle's east side.

**The rampart and the gateway.** The strip of ridge left between the cove and the circle is 2 m thick: too thin to
leave as a bank. It is built as a rampart: stone on both faces, 1.2 m high, with a hedge on top to head height. One
gateway 3.6 m wide goes through it under the ANGLERS' REST arch, with a lit lantern on the arch. The gateway is
sighted on the pier: from where players spawn you look across the cove through the arch at the Great Fire, with the
crown of lights (4 m up) showing over the hedge, whose top steps between 2.2 m and 2.8 m so the arch stays the
tallest thing on the cove. On the arch's two posts, above head height, hang the two record fish with their holders'
names, so what the best anglers have done faces the cove.

**The Landing.** In front of the gateway, at the water where today's fire is: a cobbled round 5.2 m across. It is the
stones place: a stone pile and the skip sign on its west rim, throwing north-west over open water, away from the
way in; two log seats in its north half, 3 m from the pile and clear of the path and of the pier's line to the fire;
and the Legends' Totem (the lake's legends below, the sea's above, the Kraken on top) at its north tip, where the
pier sees it. The lane from the quay arrives here past a signpost (Fire, Stones, Rockets).

**The back lane.** A second cobbled lane leaves the same meeting of lanes, comes round the ridge's foot past the Derby
stand and Thank You, and enters the circle on its south side. The triangle between the two lanes is a planted bed. The camp is a loop, not a dead end.

**The centrepiece.** The Great Fire, a stone hearth 2.1 m across, under a crown of lights on nine tall posts, with
the tarn right behind it, so from the gateway the fire is seen with its reflection.

**Measures of the circle.** Hearth, five log seats for two at 2.9 m, a clear ring 1.8 m wide all round behind the
logs, then the rim. The log ring has openings of about 2.5 m on the gateway's line, the tarn's side and the back lane's line.

**Seats (17 at the fire, 4 on the Landing).** The five logs (10), each with its instrument. Stone seat wall along the
north rim (7). The south-east rim, in front of Bram's counter, is open for 4 m.

**The boards.** Lake Legends and Deep Sea stand on the rim at floor level, each in its own gap with no seat in front,
either side of the gateway, each with its record fish above. They are read from the clear ring.

**The tarn.** The circle's east rim is a low kerb wall, knee-high, with the water behind it, so the ring keeps its
width whatever the shoreline does. The fuse post stands at the kerb's middle, 3.5 m from the nearest seat; the rocket raft is moored out on the tarn. Rockets go up from the water in front of everyone at the
fire, 6 m from the nearest seat, and show over the trees from town. The far bank is a rock bank stacked the full 2 m with reeds at its foot,
built from the boulders the lake's shore already uses: a retaining line, not a bare slope. The tarn is water like any
other: a cast lands in it and it fishes as the shore does.

**Bram's Kettle.** The host's stall stands off the back lane at the circle's south-east, under the big leafy tree
that already grows there, its counter turned to the fire across 4 m of open rim, with a lit lantern and a patch of lawn. A plaque at the
counter says "Light it at the fuse post". Bram Tinder sells
three rockets: a small one, a big one and a finale priced for the richest anglers (the prices are Oscar's).

**Edges.** Cove side: the rampart. North: the retaining wall with the hedge on top, running on to join the tarn's rock
bank, with three pines planted behind it and the second headland pine moved up beside them. East: the tarn and its rock bank. South: one run of white picket fence along
the back lane and round the stall's lawn.

**Prompts.** Seats, boards and the stall each ask the player to face them, as the casino's exit does. The fuse post,
the boards, the counter and the stone pile are each 3 m or more from one another.

**Night.** Lights that cast: the Great Fire, the arch's lantern and the stall's lantern.

**Building it.** One round level cut into the ridge, a pond, a lawn. The rampart, the gateway and the rock bank are
props standing over the terrain's slopes, which the coarse mesh cannot shape that finely. The most earthwork and the
most props of the three. Both forest trees on the site stay.

**Risks.** From the quay you see the gateway, the fire in it and the crown, not the seats. It is 10 m further from the
quay than today's fire.
