# C · Fireside Yard (round 3)

Plan: `plan-c.jpg` (lake at the top, grid 4 m). Coordinates are metres on the map (x east, z toward town). Earlier
versions are in `round1/` and `round2/`.

**What changed since round 2** (2 of 5 passed it). The yard's cove side is open down to a seat wall, so the fire shows
from the pier again, and the Derby stand moved off that line to the bank side. The hedge either side of the arch is
waist-high, so the boards and their record fish show from the quay road. The Point is drawn whole, and its centre is
the Legends' Totem where the old fire was, lit by a lantern, instead of a lamp post; it has two more seats. Bram's
counter is set back with its own apron, off the walk. Flower beds are back at the hedges' feet. The north log seat
moved clear of the north gateway's line, and the gateway lamps stand a metre off the lanes.

**The idea.** The camp is a fenced yard in town, at the quay's east end, where the quay road, the lane up to the
square and the lane down to the point already meet. It is a village fireside on the east loop, eight seconds from the
Smokehouse. The point across the lane, where the small fire is today, becomes its waterside room.

**What changed since round 1.** Passers-by now walk behind the sitters, not between them and the fire. The strand
and its jetty are gone: stones and rockets moved to the point, whose old fire retires. The circle grew from 8.4 m to
11.6 m. The boards came down to floor level. A head-high hedge closes the yard inside its fence. The gates became
open gateways.

**Site and size.** The flat grass between the quay's east end, Pip's fish racks and the wooded bank, empty today
except for two lanes. A cobbled circle 11.6 m across at (18.0, 16.6) inside a fence about 14 m across, and the Point's ring
(5.5 m, there today) 13 m to the north. No ground is
cut beyond half a metre at the bank.

**Arriving.** The quay road no longer bends away at the deck's end: it runs straight on, past the racks, under the
ANGLERS' REST arch and into the yard. An open gateway 2 m wide on the south side takes the lane up to the well
square; one on the north takes a lantern-lit lane to the Point. Lamp posts stand a metre off the lanes.

**The centrepiece.** The Great Fire, a stone hearth 2.1 m across, under a crown of lights on ten tall posts. From the
pier's root you look along the quay at the arch with the fire and its crown behind it. From the pier's end, the spawn
and the east jetty, the lines to the fire cross the yard's cove side, which is kept low for them: a seat wall on the
rim (0.5 m) and the picket fence (0.9 m), with no board on those lines. The flames stand from 0.3 m to 1.9 m.

**Sitters and walkers.** On the quay side a curved stone seat wall stands 3.4 m from the fire, in two arcs with a
2 m opening on the arch's line. Sitters sit on its fire side; behind it runs the walk, 2 m of cobbles round to both
gateways. The east loop passes through the yard without passing through the circle of friends. On the bank side there
are four log seats for two at 2.9 m, with a clear ring 1.8 m wide behind them.

**Seats (22 at the fire, 9 at the Point).** The inner seat wall (6); the four logs (8), each with its instrument; a
seat wall on the cove-side rim (4) and one on the bank-side rim (4).

**The boards.** Lake Legends and Deep Sea stand on the bank-side rim at floor level, each in its own gap with no seat
in front, facing the fire, the quay and the pier, with a forest pine standing in the wall between them and a flower bed at its foot. Each board stands 3 m tall
with its record fish above and the holder's name on a plaque sized to read from the quay road, over the waist-high
hedge (0.9 m) either side of the arch. The Derby stand is on the bank-side rim beside Lake Legends; Thank You is on
the quay-side rim, facing in, 3 m from the arch.

**Bram's Kettle.** The host's stall stands in the yard's south-west corner, a pale blue clapboard shack with a
striped awning and a lit lantern, facing the fire. It is set a metre and a half back from the rim, so its counter has its own
apron off the walk. Its back, toward the quay and the racks, has a woodpile and a hung fish. A plaque at the counter
says "Light it at the Point". The town's three counters (Marlow, Mags, Bram) stand in a row along the waterfront. Bram
Tinder sells three rockets: a small one, a big one and a finale priced for the richest anglers (the prices are
Oscar's).

**The Point.** The old cobbled ring on the headland, 5 m of lane from the north gateway, where a signpost points to
Stones and Rockets. The lane is hedged on both sides and lit by two lanterns, and from the fire you look straight
down it, through the gateway, at the Point's centre. That centre is the Legends' Totem, standing on the old hearth
where the fire was, lit by a lantern that casts light, so the jetty bench still looks across the water at a lit
landmark and the cove has one fire. The stone pile and the skip sign are on its west rim, throwing north-west toward
the cove's mouth. The rocket rack stands on a stone plinth on its lake-side rim, 3 m from the pile, with no jetty. Its
seat wall stays (seats for 5) with a hedge on the bank behind it, 3 m from the rack; two log seats stand at the lane's
end (4 more). A rope rail runs round its water side in three runs, between the rack, the pile and the lane.

**Edges.** The town's white picket fence all the way round the yard, broken only by the arch and the two gateways,
with a head-high hedge inside it on the bank side only, a waist-high hedge either side of the arch, the cove side
open above its seat wall, and flower beds at the hedges' feet and the gateways.

**Prompts.** Seats, boards and the stall each ask the player to face them, as the casino's exit does. The arch, Thank
You, the Derby stand and the counter are each 3 m or more apart. The gateways have no prompt.

**Night.** Lights that cast: the Great Fire (the old fire's light, moved), the stall's lantern and the Totem's lantern.

**What moves.** The quay road's east arm and the east ring lane are re-laid to meet at the yard; two lamp posts move
beside the gateways; the boards come over from the point. The racks, the Smokehouse and the Net Mender's stay.

**Building it.** One round level on flat ground and a ring of fence and hedge; the Point reuses the ring that is
there. New props: hearth, stall, totem, rocket rack, hedge, flat stones, plaques. The least earthwork of the three.

**Risks.** The water is not at the fire: stones and rockets are across the lane, about 13 m from the fire, in sight
of it through the north gateway. The cove's light across the water becomes a lit totem, not a fire. The yard's
cobbles come within a metre of the quay deck's end.
