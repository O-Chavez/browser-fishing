# Boardwalk and hillside (round 3)

## Summary for Oscar

**Concept.** The town turns to face the lake: a timber boardwalk runs along the waterfront and the pier grows out of its middle, so Marlow's, the Smokehouse and the boards are a few steps along the planks. Behind it the street climbs through the well square to the casino gate, where the hill begins.

**Where things are.** Spawn is mid-pier, looking at a rock islet, the T end 10 m ahead. Marlow's and the Smokehouse flank the pier root; Top Anglers, Thanks and Furthest Out stand behind the right wing. The well square, mirror on its rim, is the junction 15 m up: ramps from both wing ends, a lane west to the campfire pocket (looking back at the pier), a lane east to the Fishmonger and racks. Reed, the CHARTERS sign and the seaplane have their own beach in the east cove; worm patch, rowboat and lily pads are in the west cove under Net Mender's. Wren's Cottage and three houses stagger up the street, a bench stop halfway, to the gate.

**New.** A waterfront hub; the everyday loop (pier end, shop, smokehouse, back) is about 13 s on timber; two coves, a headland, an islet; the gate backed by a hill.

**Cost vs today.** Same 22 lamps (8 real lights) and 6 houses; one more dock rectangle; about 20 more small props; no new meshes or textures.

**Risks.** The boardwalk's land side rests on sand just under the deck; Furthest Out stands a step above the deck; Reed's beach sits under the east hill.
