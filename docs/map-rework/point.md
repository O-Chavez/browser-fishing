# The point, round 3

## Summary for Oscar

The town sits on a round point with water on three sides, and each side fishes differently: the pier runs off the tip over a short railed bridge to Lantern Rock, a lantern-lit rock islet over deep water where the big ones bite; the west side is a quiet cove with a short jetty, lily pads, reeds, the campfire and the worm patch for beginners; the south-east is Captain Reed's harbour, a sheltered basin where the seaplane moors nose to the sand. The well square sits at the foot of the pier so every walk crosses it, and the casino gate closes the road at the far end with hills behind.

Marlow's and the Smokehouse face the well from the square's lake side; Top Anglers, Thanks and Furthest Out stand together on its back rim, the wardrobe mirror opposite. Reed, his CHARTERS sign and the landing spot have their own beach 19 m from the shop. Six houses face the lake behind the square; halfway up the road a lit lamp, bench and flower bed make a stop.

New: the point with two bays, the islet, the beginner jetty, the basin, services on the square, a ~17 s loop.

Cost: 6 houses, 18 lamp posts with 8 point lights (today 22/8), 4 timber rectangles, ~65 props, one new simple mesh (the islet).

Risks: a steep headland behind the harbour and a knoll south of Reed's beach (hill noise); the 2.4 m bridge could bunch a crowd.
