# Harbour crescent, round 3

## Summary for Oscar

The town wraps a small cove like a fishing harbour: two pine-topped headlands frame the water and the pier runs straight out between them from a timber quay. The first cast looks at open lake, mountains and a rock islet; turning round shows the whole village along the waterfront.

Where things went. Marlow's Bait & Tackle and the Smokehouse flank the pier's foot on the quay, facing the water, the Fishmonger and Net Mender beside them. The Top Anglers, Thanks and Furthest Out boards hang at the quay's east end by a bench jetty; the wardrobe mirror at its west end. The well square sits up the main path with Wren's Cottage and three houses staggered around it; the casino gate closes the path. Captain Reed, his Charters sign, the seaplane and the dive landing spot have their own beach under the west headland; worm patch and rowboat by the quay's west end. The campfire sits on the east headland, seen across the water from the jetty bench.

What is new. A cove with framing headlands, a working quay with crates and lanterns, a bench jetty, a far islet, a rest stop on the walk up, and a loop that passes the boards.

Cost. Same houses, docks and point lights (8) as today; 20 lamps; about 15 more small props (rocks, reeds, pines, a bench). No new kinds of mesh.

Risks. Boards and mirror stand on the quay deck; side casts from the pier's foot are shorter than today; hill puddles from the noise terrain, as today.
