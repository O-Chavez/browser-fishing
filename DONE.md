- [x] F1, fight rework (done 2026-10-31, branch `f1-fight-rework`): started as rule set B, "Line strain", and became a tension-management fight over ten play tests with Oscar. Pond fights too.
  - The rule: keep the gauge green. In a surge (after an orange SURGE! warning) the fish pulls with a set strength, shown as a white marker; below it the fish takes line (TIGHTEN!), and holding pushes tension into the red (EASE OFF!), where a fray mark fills, faster the deeper the red. Two frays snap the line, one per stretch of red; a part-filled mark drains back, and letting go never frays. Fish above the gear also drag line out with the gauge green, less the higher you ride; the fish escapes if it takes all the line. On fish the gear can handle, holding truly green in a surge still reels in. Harder fish pull harder, move the needle faster and surge longer, though a surge builds over at least 0.4 s until far above the gear. The green sweet spot shrinks as a fish outclasses the gear, and "Too strong for your rod!" shows once its pull passes the red. Levers are in `P` (`src/game/reel.ts`).
  - Pond difficulty is a tier ladder (`reelDifficulty`, `progression.ts`): matched gear 1.2 / 1.5 / 1.6 / 1.7 / 1.8 for tiers 1-5, ×1.2 per tier above the rod and ×0.85 per tier below, with species stamina, size (×0.85-1.15), a shiny and the gloves on top. Sea difficulty is left to D1.
